## Workflow and import evidence

[AutoRFP.ai features](https://autorfp.ai/features) describes response projects, reporting, library ownership and review, and an agent connection. The rebuild does not claim these concepts are absent from AutoRFP.ai. It makes a smaller, editable record system available under an open-source licence.

[Project export and Excel Backup](https://learn.autorfp.ai/en/articles/9563240-how-to-export-projects-and-create-export-templates) documents the backup export and customer-format exports. The article does not establish stable Excel Backup headings. Our importer accepts the backup's requirement-and-response sheet after CSV conversion, with explicit header mapping. A real vendor workbook was not tested. It is not a full-account migration.

[Content library](https://autorfp.ai/features/rfp-content-library) describes current vendor content search and review. Our search is a substring query over locally stored text. It does not reproduce connected knowledge search or generate unsourced answers.
